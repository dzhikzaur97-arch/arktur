**Full description (up to 4000 characters):**

ARKTUR is a big EVE-style space sandbox that fits in your phone and works offline.

You start as the pilot of a small frigate, the «Iskra», at Arktur Prime station. Where you go next is up to you.

★ 28 star systems in five regions — from the safe Core to Deep Space, where income is four times higher but there is no law.
★ Over 30 ships: mining barges, battleships, drone carriers, freighters and faction flagships. Slot-based fitting and module upgrades up to level 5.
★ Ore mining, trading, refining at your own factory and production from blueprints.
★ Blight pirates, pirate bases with five waves of guards and fortresses in the most dangerous sectors.
★ A war of three factions for the border systems: pick a side, take front-line tasks and capture systems.
★ Wingmen — AI pilots who fly beside you: a fighter, a miner and a medic. Train them up to level 7 and upgrade their ships.
★ Planet landings: 9 biomes, creatures, weather, day and night, and your own base with a resource extractor.
★ World events: a moon-sized Precursor Colossus and the «Leviafan» dreadnought.
★ Wormholes into unexplored systems that collapse after 45 minutes.
★ Two story campaigns, a pilot career, 25 achievements and titles.

The game is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. It collects nothing: saves stay on your device and can be exported to a file.
Available in English and Russian.
